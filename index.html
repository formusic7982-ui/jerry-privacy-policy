1. About Jerry and this policy

Jerry is a private professional-content assistant developed for Bhagwati Constructions. It is intended to help an authorized user draft and review LinkedIn content for their personal profile and company page. This policy describes how information is handled in connection with Jerry. It does not cover all other business activities of Bhagwati Constructions.

LinkedIn sign-in: This policy covers Jerry’s optional, read-only LinkedIn sign-in check on Cloudflare Workers. When enabled, the check identifies the personal LinkedIn account you choose to authorize. The ChatGPT plugin is used for drafting and review. The sign-in check does not publish content or access or change company pages.

2. Information involved in using Jerry

You may voluntarily provide information in ChatGPT when using Jerry, such as your professional biography, company information, draft posts, project descriptions, profile links, instructions, and other content you choose to share. Do not include passwords, one-time verification codes, session cookies, or other sensitive credentials.

If you choose to sign in, Jerry requests LinkedIn’s openid and profile permissions. Jerry validates the LinkedIn account identifier and displays the account name returned by LinkedIn. LinkedIn may return other basic profile fields with these permissions. Jerry discards those fields and does not request the email permission.

3. How information is used

Information you provide is used to help prepare and improve professional content, suggest profile wording, and review proposed communications. Content prepared by Jerry is a draft for your review; creating a draft does not publish it on LinkedIn.

Jerry is not intended to scrape LinkedIn, send unsolicited bulk messages, automate connection requests, or generate artificial engagement. No future feature should publish content or make account changes without your approval.

4. LinkedIn permissions and your choices

The sign-in check uses LinkedIn’s official consent page. You can decline access and continue to use Jerry’s drafting features. The sign-in check requests personal profile access only. Publishing permissions and company-page permissions are outside this sign-in check.

You can manage third-party app permissions in your LinkedIn account settings. Revoking permission may stop connected features from working.

5. Service providers and sharing

When Jerry is used inside ChatGPT, the information you submit through ChatGPT is handled by OpenAI under the terms, privacy notice, and account settings applicable to that service. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Bhagwati Constructions does not sell LinkedIn account information obtained through Jerry. Cloudflare Workers hosts the sign-in check and processes its requests. The sign-in check communicates with LinkedIn to verify the selected account. It does not send the result, profile data, or OAuth tokens to ChatGPT.

6. Storage, security, and retention

Jerry handles LinkedIn OAuth tokens in server memory for the duration of the sign-in check and does not save them in a database, browser cookie, or application log. Jerry uses encrypted browser cookies to complete sign-in. The sign-in cookie expires after ten minutes, and the result cookie expires after one minute or is cleared when the result is displayed. The result cookie contains the display name if LinkedIn provides it. It does not contain a LinkedIn token or member identifier.

Information submitted within ChatGPT is subject to the retention and deletion controls applicable to your ChatGPT account and service.

7. Access, correction, and deletion requests

To ask about personal information that Bhagwati Constructions may hold through Jerry, or to request its correction or deletion, contact jayraj.bhagwati@outlook.com. Requests will be reviewed under applicable law and relevant service requirements.

For information held by ChatGPT, use the privacy and data controls provided by OpenAI. You can revoke Jerry’s access in LinkedIn’s settings.

8. Third-party websites

LinkedIn and ChatGPT are separate services with their own terms and privacy practices. For more information about LinkedIn, see LinkedIn's Privacy Policy. Links to other websites are governed by those sites' own policies.

9. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy if Jerry's functions, data practices, providers, or legal obligations change. The updated policy will show a new effective date. Any material new collection or use of LinkedIn information will be described before that functionality is enabled.
